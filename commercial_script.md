# MyLuckySol - 60-Second Commercial Script

**Theme:** Minimalist, High-Stakes, Provably Fair.
**Tone:** Cinematic, sleek, and confident.

---

### Scene 1 (0-10s): The Hook
**Visual:** Close-up of a sleek Solana logo pulsing in the center of a dark, obsidian-textured background. Golden particles begin to swirl around it.
**Action:** The camera slowly zooms out to reveal the MyLuckySol logo.
**Voice-Over:** "In the world of chance, trust is the only currency that matters."

### Scene 2 (10-20s): The Problem & Solution
**Visual:** Split screen. Left side shows cluttered, traditional casino UI. Right side shows the clean, dark-themed MyLuckySol 'Play' interface.
**Action:** The left side fades into a "Powered by HMAC-SHA256" technical overlay on the right.
**Voice-Over:** "No more black boxes. No more guessing. MyLuckySol brings high-stakes gaming to the Solana blockchain with total transparency."

### Scene 3 (20-30s): The Modes
**Visual:** Rapid, minimalist cards sliding in: "1v1", "2-Round", "3-Round", "4-Round". 
**Action:** Icons for 2, 4, 8, and 16 players appear and disappear in sync with the beat.
**Voice-Over:** "From head-to-head battles to sixteen-player tournaments. Choose your mode. Choose your wager."

### Scene 4 (30-40s): The Adrenaline
**Visual:** A 10-second countdown timer appearing on screen, ticking down from 10 to 1. The background music intensifies.
**Action:** The numbers glow with a Solana-gradient (Purple to Green).
**Voice-Over:** "Simple mechanics. Intense moments. 90% of the pool goes straight to the winner. Automatically."

### Scene 5 (40-50s): WAGA Rewards
**Visual:** A 3D gold WAGA token spinning. Text appears: "100x Entry Match" and "1000x Win Bonus".
**Action:** The token 'bursts' into smaller tokens that fly toward the 'Profile' icon.
**Voice-Over:** "Win or lose, you're always earning. Get matched in WAGA tokens for every play, and multiply your rewards when you take the crown."

### Scene 6 (50-60s): Call to Action
**Visual:** The MyLuckySol logo again, with a "Connect Wallet" button pulsing below it. "A TryUnity Foundation DApp" appears in small, elegant text at the bottom.
**Action:** Website URL fades in: myluckysol.replit.app
**Voice-Over:** "MyLuckySol. Provably Fair. Unquestionably Fast. Test your luck today."
