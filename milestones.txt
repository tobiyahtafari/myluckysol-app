# MYLUCKYSOL TREASURY GIVEAWAY MILESTONE

This milestone is based on the accumulation of 250 SOL into the Treasury Giveaway Wallet.
Contribution: 1% of every game's total wager pool.

## Target: 250 SOL Giveaway Pot

The giveaway will trigger once the Giveaway Wallet reaches 250 SOL.

### Base Calculation (Minimum Case)
Based on the cheapest possible game (0.01 SOL 1v1):
- **Giveaway Fee per Game**: 0.0002 SOL
- **Total Games Needed**: 1,250,000 games

### Acceleration Factors
The pot will grow significantly faster when higher wagers and more players are involved:
- **0.1 SOL 2-Round**: Triggered in 62,500 games
- **1.0 SOL 3-Round**: Triggered in 3,125 games
- **10.0 SOL 4-Round**: Triggered in 157 games

## Winner Selection
Upon reaching the 250 SOL milestone, the pot will be distributed as follows:

### Low Stakes (0.01 - 0.1 SOL Tiers)
- **Selection**: Longest Streak Leaderboard
- **Distribution**: Top 10 players split 125 SOL (50% of the pot)

### High Stakes (1.0 - 10.0 SOL Tiers)
- **Selection**: Luckiest Player Leaderboard
- **Distribution**: Top 10 players split 125 SOL (50% of the pot)

---
*Note: This system scales automatically with platform activity. More high-stakes games mean faster milestones and larger frequent rewards.*
