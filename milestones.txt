MYLUCKYSOL MILESTONE & GIVEAWAY SYSTEM (FINAL)
Milestones are based on TOTAL GAMES PLAYED per mode combination.
Fee Structure: 9% Treasury | 1% Giveaway Wallet (10% Total)

WINNER SELECTION LOGIC:
- 0.01 & 0.1 SOL Games: Winners determined by LONGEST STREAK Leaderboard.
- 1 & 10 SOL Games:    Winners determined by LUCKIEST PLAYER Leaderboard.

DISTRIBUTION:
- 1v1 & 2-Round: Top 10 players on the respective leaderboard split the reward.
- 3-Round & 4-Round: Top 5 players on the respective leaderboard split the reward.
