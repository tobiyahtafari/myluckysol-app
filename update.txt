# UPDATES & REFINEMENTS: MYLUCKYSOL PHASE 2

This document serves as the pre-implementation blueprint for the "God Mode" and "Million Game Jackpot" expansion.

## 1. GOD STREAK & STREAK BREAKER LOGIC
- **God Streak Trigger**: 50 in 1,000,000 chance (0.005%).
- **God Streak Length**: Randomly predetermined between 26 and 333 games. 
- **Hidden Length**: The remaining streak count is hidden from all users, including the God.
- **God Power**: 95% weighted win chance in all games.
- **Streak Breaker Trigger**: 250,000 in 1,000,000 chance (25%).
- **Breaker Condition**: Can ONLY be triggered by a user when facing a player currently on a God Streak.
- **Breaker Effect**: Strips the God's 95% advantage. The "Giant Slayer" gets a 75% win chance for that game.
- **Normal Games**: All other games remain pure 1/N random selection based on seeds.

## 2. 1 MILLION GAME LEADERBOARD
- **Location**: Dedicated page "Million Game Giveaway".
- **Content**: 
    - Real-time Jackpot breakdown in SOL and USD.
    - Reward calculations for all top 10 positions (Luck & Streaks) based on current % split.
- **Scope**: Tracks the top 10 Luck Scores and longest Streaks.
- **Timeframes**:
    - **All-Time**: 1 to 1,000,000 (Historical records).
    - **Current Cycle**: Recent progress within the current 1M game block.
- **Luck Score**: Winnings / Wager ratio.
- **Streak**: Consecutive wins without a loss.

## 3. CHAT & NOTIFICATIONS RESTRUCTURE
- **Mobile Layout (Horizontal Split)**:
    - Top: Chat Interface.
    - Bottom: Live Notifications.
- **Desktop Layout (Vertical Split)**:
    - Left: Chat Interface.
    - Right: Live Notifications.
- **Notification Behavior**: On the Chat page, standard pop-up notifications are disabled and integrated directly into the split-view sections.
- **Social Features**:
    - **Tipping**: Users can tip other members using their Wallet Address or Username.
- **Visuals**:
    - **God Streak**: Special fire animation around the user's icon.
    - **Streak Breaker**: Special water animation around the user's icon when triggered.

## 4. MILLION GAME GIVEAWAY (THE JACKPOT)
- **Contribution**: 9% Treasury | 1% Giveaway Wallet (10% Total Fee).
- **Minimum Jackpot**: Always display **200 SOL** as the floor (even at game 1).
- **Jackpot Growth**: Every game mode and wager (0.1, 1, 10 SOL) increases the pot significantly beyond the 200 SOL floor.
- **Trigger**: Reaching the 1,000,000th game in the cycle.
- **The Split**: The jackpot is split 50/50 between the Top 10 Luck Scores and Top 10 Streaks.
- **Tiered Payouts (for both leaderboards)**:

    - **Natural Breaker**: If a normal user (no breaker triggered) breaks a God Streak, they receive **5x** the 1000x WAGA win bonus.

## POTENTIAL FLAWS & RECOMMENDED FIXES

### A. God Streak "Camping"
- **Flaw**: A God might stop playing to "preserve" their streak for the leaderboard.
- **Fix**: Implement a **72-hour Activity Requirement**. If a God doesn't play a game within 72 hours, their streak is naturally "broken" by the system.

### B. Chat Spam / Tipping Abuse
- **Flaw**: Bots or malicious users could spam the chat or use tipping for money laundering.
- **Fix**: 
    - Require a **minimum wager history** (e.g., 0.1 SOL total) to use Chat/Tipping.
    - Implement a small fee (e.g., 1% or fixed 0.001 SOL) on tips to discourage micro-spam.

### C. The 1M Game Floor
- **Practice**: Always display the **200 SOL Minimum** from Game 1. Even if the actual accumulation is lower early on, the 200 SOL floor is guaranteed by the 1M game cycle (based on 0.01 SOL 1v1).
- **Growth**: Display the real-time value once it exceeds 200 SOL due to higher-stakes play.

### D. Provable Fairness Transparency
- **Flaw**: God Streaks look like "rigging" to new users.
- **Fix**: The Fairness page must explicitly explain the God/Breaker triggers and show how the hash derived the event.
